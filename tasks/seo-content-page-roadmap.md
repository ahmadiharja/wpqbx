## Content Rules

- Every page needs one primary keyword, 3-6 secondary keywords, title tag, meta description, H1, FAQ, and internal links.
- Product pages should link to relevant solution pages, resources, store, support, and request demo.
- Solution pages should link to at least two products and two resources.
- Comparison pages must be balanced and useful; avoid shallow competitor attacks.
- Legal pages must be reviewed by a qualified human before production use.
- WPML: translate pages/posts/CPT entries with WPML and custom fields with ACFML.
