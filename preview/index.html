Content lives in WordPress, not hardcoded templates.

What does the importer create?+

Pages, product CPT entries, resource CPT entries, blog/news posts, taxonomies, menus, front-page settings, and SEO meta.

Can it be translated?+

Yes. Use WPML post translation for pages and CPT entries, ACFML for fields, and WPML String Translation for registered seed strings.

Which agents were saved?+

content-strategist, seo-architect, wordpress-importer, wpml-localization, theme-maintainer, and page-progress-tracker are saved in the codebase under the agents directory.
